# ASTRA — mindmap notes

Transcribed from the original planning mindmap. This is early ideation: where it
conflicts with `docs/brief.md` or the Figma frames in `design/`, those win.

## Origins
- Initial idea: Spotify-inspired portfolio project — personalised music dashboard, search for artists and albums, genre and mood filters, personal library, interactive listening statistics.
- Concept: celestial theme — cosmic, dreamy, mystical; exploring the universe through music. "Discovering the soundtrack to your inner world."
- Inspired by: Mesmerize app, Soundverse, The Pattern, Ethereya.
- Name ideas: Astra (chosen), Luna, Orbit.

## Feature ideas
- **Mood-based discovery quiz → "visual interactive instead of quiz"**: a "sun in centre" with "orbiting moods" (inspired by a mood-tracker dial). Prompt: *"What does your universe sound like today?"*
- **Cosmic identity questions** (feed the quiz):
  - Do you listen to lyrics, rhythm or melodies first?
  - Do you gravitate towards music that calms you or elevates you?
  - What kind of atmosphere draws you in?
  - What time of day do you feel most connected to music?
- **Results** generate recommended artists, genres and playlists → *Music identity profile: "Discover your sound / Your cosmic identity".*
- **Genre and mood filters** → "Your moon": choose your current mood or emotional state and discover music to match. Astrology link idea: add sun, moon and rising for themed music around your top 3 signs. *(Needs a decision — the brief says Frequency is about musical taste, not personality assessment.)*
- **Daily music discovery** → "Your daily alignment", "Constellations" (linked discoveries).
- **Saved music** → "Your orbit". "Your frequency" as the identity name.
- **Now playing** screen with a **visualiser option** (circular progress arc, glowing artwork).
- Visual motifs: orbital lines and subtle constellations; nebula imagery; flowing wave forms.

## Decisions (2026-11-15)
- **Your Frequency = a mix**: standard questions plus a visual, interactive "orbiting moods" step.
- **Astrology (sun / moon / rising) is out** — Frequency stays about musical taste.
